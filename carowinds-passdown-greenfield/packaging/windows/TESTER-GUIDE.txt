Carowinds Operations — Tester Guide
====================================

Thanks for testing this app. You do not need any technical background to do this —
just follow the steps below in order. If anything looks wrong or doesn't match what
this guide describes, see "If something goes wrong" at the end.

1. Extract the ZIP
-------------------
Right-click the ZIP file and choose "Extract All...". Pick a normal folder you can
write to (Desktop, Documents, a USB drive). Do NOT run the app from inside the ZIP
without extracting it first — Windows will complain or the app may not behave
correctly.

2. Launch the app
------------------
Open the extracted folder and double-click Carowinds.Passdown.Desktop.exe. The app
should open in a few seconds. Nothing else needs to be installed first.

3. The Dashboard (default screen)
-------------------------------------
The app opens on the "Dashboard" — a one-screen summary of today's operation. At the
top of the window you'll see five sections: "Dashboard", "Operations Calendar",
"Associate Passdown", "Operational Notes", and "History". Dashboard is selected by
default. On it you should see:

- Park Status — today's hours (or Closed/Not Published/Unavailable, whichever
  applies).
- Passdown Status — whether today's passdown has a draft, and an "Open Passdown"
  button.
- Today at a Glance — attendance, pre-solds, Early Ride Time, and the Daily Feature.
- Passdown Notes preview and an "Open Notes" button.
- Active Operational Notes — a short list of today's relevant persistent operational
  notes (see step 5), separate from the Passdown Notes above.
- Latest Finalized — the most recent finalized (past) passdown, with an
  "Open History" button.
- Data Status — a summary of how current the underlying park data is, plus a
  "Check Online" button.

There is no Refresh button: the Dashboard updates itself whenever something is saved
on your computer (for example attendance or a gate time typed on Associate Passdown).
A small line near the top says "Official data checked <time>" — the app checks
Carowinds' official sources by itself when it starts and every four hours while it
is open. "Check Online" (in the Data Status card) checks right away — see step 11.

4. Open Passdown / Open Notes / Open History
------------------------------------------------
From the Dashboard, click "Open Passdown" — it should take you to the Associate
Passdown screen's Overview. Go back to Dashboard, click "Open Notes" — it should
take you to Associate Passdown's Notes area instead. Go back to Dashboard again and
click "Open History" — it should take you to the History section. None of these
should lose anything you've typed on the Associate side, and none of them should
change which date Associate Passdown has selected.

5. Operations Calendar
------------------------
Click the "Operations Calendar" section at the top of the window. This is a
month-grid view for planning and jumping to a specific date — it doesn't let you
edit anything itself.

- You should see a full month grid with today's date visibly marked, plus a
  "Selected Date" panel on the right showing park hours, passdown status (No
  Draft/Draft/Finalized), and any relevant Operational Notes for the selected date.
- Click "‹ Prev" / "Next ›" to move between months, and "Today" to jump back to the
  current month. Click a date that belongs to the previous or next month (shown
  dimmed at the edges of the grid) — the calendar should move to that date's own
  month and select it.
- Use "‹ Prev Day" / "Next Day ›" in the Selected Date panel to step one day at a
  time, including across a month boundary (e.g. from the last day of a month to the
  1st of the next).
- Click a date with a Closed park day, a Custom Hours day, or a Private Buyout day,
  if your sample data has one — the Selected Date panel and the day cell itself
  should both reflect it (never just "Open").
- Click "Open Passdown" — it should take you to Associate Passdown's Overview for
  that exact date. Go back to Operations Calendar (your selected date and displayed
  month should be exactly as you left them).
- Click "Open History" — it is only clickable once the selected date has a
  finalized passdown; try it on a date you know is finalized and confirm History
  opens with that same date already selected. On a date with no finalized passdown,
  the button should be visibly disabled (not hidden).
- Click "Open Operational Notes" — it should take you to the Operational Notes
  Manager.

6. Operational Notes Manager
--------------------------------
Click the "Operational Notes" section at the top of the window. This is a separate,
supervisor-facing list of notes that can span multiple days (unlike a daily
passdown's own notes).

- Click "+ New Note", fill in a made-up Title, pick a Category and Priority, leave
  "Until resolved" checked, and save. It should appear in the list.
- Select it and click "Edit" — change the title slightly and save; the change
  should show immediately.
- Use the filter tabs (All / Active / Upcoming / Expired / Resolved) and the
  category/priority filters — the list should update instantly with no internet
  needed.
- Click "Resolve" on your test note, confirm the prompt — it should disappear from
  the Active tab but still be visible under "Resolved" (there is no delete button;
  resolved notes are kept, not removed).

7. Pick a date
---------------
On the Associate Passdown screen, use the date picker (and the ◀ / ▶ arrows) near
the top of the window to select a date. Changing the date should feel instant — it
does not need the internet.

8. Enter some sample information
----------------------------------
Type a few things into the fields on the page — for example, a projected attendance
number, an operational note, or a Daily Fun note. Use made-up/sample values only;
do not enter anything private or real.

9. Let it save
---------------
The app saves automatically a moment after you stop typing. Watch the small status
chip near the top-right of the window — it should change to say your changes were
saved.

10. Close and reopen
---------------------
Close the app completely, then double-click Carowinds.Passdown.Desktop.exe again to
reopen it. Go back to the same date and confirm your sample information from step 8
is still there.

11. Try "Check Online"
------------------------
Click the "Check Online" button (on Associate Passdown, or on the Dashboard's Data
Status card). This checks the official Carowinds sources for the selected date. It
may take a few seconds — while it runs you'll see a small progress indicator, and
the app stays usable. When it finishes, a short status message appears. This step
needs an internet connection — if it fails and you have no internet, that's
expected, not a bug.

12. Include a persistent operational note in a passdown
------------------------------------------------------------
This checks that Operational Notes are never added to a passdown automatically —
only when a supervisor explicitly chooses to include one.

- In Operational Notes (step 6), create a test note with an Effective From date
  that covers the date you picked in step 7.
- Go to Associate Passdown's Notes area for that same date. Your test note should
  appear under "available" suggestions — NOT already included.
- Click "Add" on it. It should now appear under the included/selected notes for
  this passdown.
- Go back to Operational Notes and edit that note's title. Return to Associate
  Passdown's Notes area — the included copy should still show the ORIGINAL title,
  plus a note that the source has changed, and a "Replace with Current Version"
  button.
- Click "Replace with Current Version" — the included copy should now show the
  updated title.
- Click "Remove from this passdown" — it should disappear from the included list
  and reappear as an available suggestion. Confirm in Operational Notes that the
  original note itself still exists and is unaffected.

13. Create a PDF
-----------------
Click "Create PDF". A save dialog will appear — choose anywhere you like (e.g. your
Desktop) and save it. Open the saved PDF afterward and confirm it looks readable,
matches what you entered, and (if you added one in step 12) shows the included
operational note's text.

14. Turn on automatic daily refresh (optional)
-----------------------------------------------
In the extracted folder, double-click "Enable Automatic Refresh.cmd". A small window
will briefly appear, tell you whether it worked, and wait for a key press before
closing. No sign-in or admin prompt should appear.

15. Double-check it's on (optional)
--------------------------------------
If you're comfortable with it, open Windows' "Task Scheduler" app and look for a
task named "Carowinds Passdown Daily Refresh". Seeing it there confirms step 14
worked. This step is optional — most testers can skip it.

16. Turn automatic refresh back off
--------------------------------------
Double-click "Disable Automatic Refresh.cmd" in the same folder. Again, a small
window reports what happened.

17. If you move the app folder
---------------------------------
You can move or copy the extracted folder anywhere else at any time — your saved
information is not stored inside that folder, so it isn't lost. If you had turned on
automatic refresh (step 14), double-click "Enable Automatic Refresh.cmd" again from
the NEW location afterward so the schedule points at the right place.

18. Export diagnostics
-------------------------
If you want to help troubleshoot something, click "Export Diagnostics" (next to
"Create PDF"). Choose where to save the file — it creates one ZIP file containing
version info, app health information, and recent activity logs. It does NOT include
anything you typed into the app (no drafts, no notes, no operational notes content)
and does not include your saved database.

19. Passdown History
-----------------------
Near the top of the window, there's a "History" section (also reachable via
"Open History" from the Dashboard or Operations Calendar). This is where finalized
(past) passdowns live — a read-only record of exactly what associates saw for a
given day.

- A passdown finalizes automatically the morning after it happens (after 6:00 AM
  the following day) — you don't need to do anything to make this happen, and there
  is no button to finalize one manually.
- Once a passdown is finalized, it never changes again. History does not have edit,
  delete, or "undo" controls of any kind — that's intentional.
- Click any date in the History list to see its details (park hours, attendance,
  products, notes, etc.) exactly as they were finalized.
- "Open Final PDF" opens the exact PDF an associate would have received that day.
  "Save PDF Copy" lets you save that same exact PDF somewhere else. Both use the
  original stored PDF — the app never regenerates or recreates it.
- Some very old records may not have a stored PDF available; if so, the PDF buttons
  won't appear for that record, and that's expected, not a bug.
- Switching between "Dashboard", "Operations Calendar", "Associate Passdown",
  "Operational Notes", and "History" never loses your in-progress work on the
  Associate side, and never changes what Operations Calendar has selected.

20. If something goes wrong
------------------------------
- Note exactly what you were doing and what you expected to happen instead.
- Click "Export Diagnostics" (step 18) and save the resulting ZIP file.
- Send both the diagnostics ZIP and your written notes to whoever asked you to test
  this app.
- Do not delete anything under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Carowinds Passdown while
  troubleshooting — that folder holds your saved data and is separate from the app
  folder you extracted.

That's the full test. Thank you!
